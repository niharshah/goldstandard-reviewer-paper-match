<Title: Escape saddle points by a simple gradient-descent based algorithm
Abstract: Escaping saddle points is a central research topic in nonconvex optimization. In this paper, we propose a simple gradient-based algorithm such that for a smooth function f : Rn ! R, it outputs an ✏-approximate second-order stationary point in Õ(log n/✏1.75) iterations. Compared to the previous state-of-the-art algorithms by Jin et al. with Õ(log n/✏2) or Õ(log n/✏1.75) iterations, our algorithm is polynomially better in terms of log n and matches their complexities in terms of 1/✏. For the stochastic setting, our algorithm outputs an ✏-approximate second-order stationary point in Õ(log n/✏4) iterations. Technically, our main contribution is an idea of implementing a robust Hessian power method using only gradients, which can find negative curvature near saddle points and achieve the polynomial speedup in log n compared to the perturbed gradient descent methods. Finally, we also perform numerical experiments that support our results. >
